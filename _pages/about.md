<p class="hero-lead lang-en">Afterglow materials for sensing, imaging and therapy.</p>
<p class="hero-lead lang-zh">余辉发光材料：传感、成像与治疗。</p>

<p class="hero-bio lang-en">I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Chemistry and Molecular Engineering at Nanjing Tech University, where I started in July 2024. I work on luminescent materials that keep emitting after the excitation light is switched off, and on putting that delayed light to use in sensing, imaging and therapy.</p>
<p class="hero-bio lang-zh">我是南京工业大学化学与分子工程学院的博士研究生，2024 年 7 月入学。我研究激发光关闭后仍能持续发光的材料，并把这种延迟发光用于传感、成像与治疗。</p>

<h3><span class="lang-en">Organic afterglow for tumor imaging and therapy</span><span class="lang-zh">有机余辉：肿瘤成像与治疗</span></h3>
<p class="lang-en">Afterglow is collected after the excitation stops, so the signal is free of tissue autofluorescence. I am developing organic afterglow materials that bring this advantage to tumor imaging and pair it with therapy.</p>
<p class="lang-zh">余辉在激发停止后采集，可以避开组织自发荧光的干扰。我在开发有机余辉材料，把这一优势用于肿瘤成像，并与治疗结合。</p>
