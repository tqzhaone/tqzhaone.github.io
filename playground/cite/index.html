DOI 转参考文献DOI to reference

粘贴 DOI（一行一个，也可以直接贴一大段文字或链接，会自动找出里面的 DOI），生成 GB/T 7714、APA、ACS、BibTeX 或 RIS 格式。文献信息来自 Crossref。

Paste DOIs (one per line, or any text or links with DOIs in them) and get references in GB/T 7714, APA, ACS, BibTeX or RIS. The metadata comes from Crossref.

    GB/T 7714 按 2015 版顺序编码制整理：西文作者姓全大写、名缩写不加点，超过三位作者写前三位加「et al」（中文文献加「等」）。复制时会带上斜体等格式，粘贴到 Word 里也能保留。投稿前请再按期刊要求核对一遍。GB/T 7714 follows the 2015 numeric style: surnames in capitals, initials without points, the first three authors then “et al”. Copying keeps italics, so they survive a paste into Word. Check against the journal's own rules before you submit.
