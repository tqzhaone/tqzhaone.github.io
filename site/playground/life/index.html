生命游戏Game of Life

每个格子只看身边的八个邻居：死格子周围恰好三个活的，就活过来；活格子周围两个或三个活的，就继续活。死去的格子会留一会儿余辉。在格子上点按或拖动可以画。

Each cell looks only at its eight neighbours: a dead cell with exactly three live ones comes alive; a live cell with two or three stays alive. Cells that die keep glowing for a moment. Click or drag to draw.
