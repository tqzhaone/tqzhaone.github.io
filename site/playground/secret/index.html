余辉密信Afterglow secret

一句话用两种发光材料印在一起：灯亮时读到的是伪装，关灯后短余辉的点马上熄灭，留下来的余辉才是真正想说的。

One sheet, two phosphors. Under the lamp you read the decoy; switch it off and the short-lived dots die at once, while the afterglow spells what was really meant.

有人给你寄来一封余辉密信。按住「激发」，松开之后，看看留下的是什么。Someone sent you an afterglow secret. Hold “Excite”, let go, and see what stays lit.
