怎么算的How it works

模型是 y = y₀ + Σ Aᵢ·exp(−t/τᵢ)。寿命 τᵢ 用 Levenberg–Marquardt 法迭代，每组寿命下的振幅 Aᵢ 和背景 y₀ 直接用线性最小二乘解出来（变量投影法），并从多个初始值出发，取残差最小的结果。误差是协方差矩阵对角元的平方根（一倍标准差）。

The model is y = y₀ + Σ Aᵢ·exp(−t/τᵢ). The lifetimes τᵢ are found by Levenberg–Marquardt; for each set of lifetimes the amplitudes Aᵢ and the background y₀ are solved exactly by linear least squares (variable projection), and the fit starts from several initial guesses and keeps the best. Errors are the square roots of the covariance matrix's diagonal (one standard deviation).

平均寿命用强度加权：τavg = Σ Aᵢτᵢ² / Σ Aᵢτᵢ；「占比」是每个分量对积分强度的贡献 Aᵢτᵢ / Σ Aⱼτⱼ。长余辉的衰减往往不是严格的指数形式，多指数拟合给出的是描述曲线的有效寿命。

The average lifetime is intensity-weighted: τavg = Σ Aᵢτᵢ² / Σ Aᵢτᵢ; each component's share is its part of the integrated intensity, Aᵢτᵢ / Σ Aⱼτⱼ. Persistent-luminescence decays are often not truly exponential: a multi-exponential fit gives effective lifetimes that describe the curve.
