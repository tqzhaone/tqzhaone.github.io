猜温度Guess the temperature

Cr³⁺ 的发光里有一条很窄的锐线和一个很宽的宽带，温度越高，宽带相对锐线越强。两者之比就是一支温度计。看光谱，猜温度，一局五轮。

Cr³⁺ emits a sharp line and a broad band, and the hotter it gets, the stronger the band becomes against the line. Their ratio is a thermometer. Read the spectrum, guess the temperature: five rounds a game.

为什么比值能测温Why a ratio reads temperature

发宽带的能级 ⁴T₂ 比发锐线的 ²E 高出一点点（ΔE）。温度越高，越多的电子被热激发到 ⁴T₂，宽带就越强。两者之比服从玻尔兹曼分布：

The level behind the broad band (⁴T₂) sits a little above the one behind the sharp line (²E), by ΔE. The hotter it is, the more electrons are thermally lifted into ⁴T₂, and the stronger the band. The ratio follows a Boltzmann law:

R = Iband / Iline = A · exp(−ΔE / kT)

用比值而不用单一强度，是因为比值不受激发光强弱、探测距离和材料浓度的影响。这里的参数是示意用的。

A ratio, not a single intensity, because it doesn't care how strong the excitation is, how far the detector sits or how much material there is. The numbers here are for illustration.

玻尔兹曼图Boltzmann plot

ln R 对 1/T 是一条直线，斜率就是 −ΔE/k。实心点是每一轮的真实温度，空心点是你的猜测，两点之间的横向距离就是误差。

ln R against 1/T is a straight line with slope −ΔE/k. Filled dots are each round's true temperature, hollow ones your guesses: the gap between them is your error.
